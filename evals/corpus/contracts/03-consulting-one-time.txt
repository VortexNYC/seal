PROFESSIONAL CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: February 10, 2026

PARTIES

This Professional Consulting Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Apex Strategy Group, LLC, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1 Liberty Plaza, Suite 3200, New York, NY 10006 ("Consultant"), and

Kestrel Manufacturing Corp., a Michigan corporation with its principal place of business at 8800 Industrial Parkway, Grand Rapids, MI 49512 ("Client").

SCOPE OF WORK

Consultant shall deliver a comprehensive operational efficiency assessment and transformation roadmap for Client's Grand Rapids manufacturing facility. Deliverables include: (i) a two-week on-site assessment involving structured interviews with department heads and floor supervisors; (ii) process mapping and bottleneck analysis across receiving, production, and shipping workflows; (iii) a written findings report with quantified improvement opportunities; and (iv) a prioritized 90-day implementation roadmap. All deliverables shall be submitted within forty-five (45) days of the Effective Date.

FEES AND PAYMENT

Client shall pay Consultant a flat project fee of twenty-two thousand five hundred dollars ($22,500.00) for the full scope of work described herein. In addition, Client shall reimburse Consultant for pre-approved travel and lodging expenses not to exceed two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500.00), which shall be invoiced separately with supporting receipts. The project fee invoice shall be issued upon execution of this Agreement and is due upon receipt.

Payment shall be made by ACH transfer or company check. Consultant reserves the right to withhold delivery of the final written report until payment has been received in full. No late fee applies provided payment is made within ten (10) business days of receipt of invoice.

All amounts are denominated in United States dollars.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Consultant agrees to hold all information disclosed by Client in connection with this engagement in strict confidence and not to disclose such information to any third party without Client's prior written consent.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
