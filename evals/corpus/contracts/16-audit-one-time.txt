CYBERSECURITY AUDIT SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: March 20, 2026

PARTIES

This Cybersecurity Audit Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

RedVector Security Consulting, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1775 Tysons Boulevard, Suite 400, McLean, VA 22102 ("Firm"), and

Cascade Community Bank, N.A., a nationally chartered bank with its principal place of business at 620 SW 5th Avenue, Portland, OR 97204 ("Bank").

SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT

Firm shall conduct a comprehensive cybersecurity maturity assessment of Bank's information systems and technology infrastructure, including: (i) external network reconnaissance and vulnerability scanning; (ii) web application assessment of Bank's internet-facing portals and mobile banking application; (iii) internal network segmentation review; (iv) active directory and identity access management review; (v) review of Bank's security policies against FFIEC Cybersecurity Assessment Tool (CAT) standards; and (vi) tabletop exercise simulating a ransomware incident response. Firm shall deliver a final assessment report with executive summary, technical findings, risk ratings, and a remediation roadmap within fifteen (15) business days of the conclusion of on-site assessment activities.

FEES AND PAYMENT

Bank shall pay Firm a fixed engagement fee of thirty-seven thousand five hundred dollars ($37,500.00) for the full scope of services described herein. The engagement fee shall be invoiced upon execution of this Agreement and is due within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date. All amounts are denominated in United States dollars.

If payment is not received within five (5) days of the due date, a late fee of one percent (1%) of the outstanding balance per month shall apply until the account is paid in full.

Travel and accommodation expenses associated with on-site activities shall be reimbursed separately at cost, not to exceed three thousand dollars ($3,000.00), invoiced within thirty (30) days of the engagement conclusion and due on receipt.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Given the sensitive nature of the findings, Firm agrees to maintain strict confidentiality and to limit disclosure of any findings to Bank personnel with a documented need to know.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Virginia.
