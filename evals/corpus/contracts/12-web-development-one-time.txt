WEB DEVELOPMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: January 20, 2026

PARTIES

This Web Development Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Ironworks Digital LLC, an Arizona limited liability company with its principal place of business at 2355 E Camelback Road, Suite 300, Phoenix, AZ 85016 ("Developer"), and

Sunrise Orthodontics Group, P.C., an Arizona professional corporation with its principal place of business at 7878 N 16th Street, Suite 210, Phoenix, AZ 85020 ("Client").

SCOPE OF WORK

Developer shall design and develop a responsive, mobile-first website for Client's multi-location orthodontic practice. Deliverables include: (i) custom website design in Figma with two revision rounds; (ii) front-end and CMS implementation using WordPress and Elementor Pro; (iii) integration of online appointment booking widget (NexHealth); (iv) SEO on-page setup including metadata, schema markup, and sitemap submission; (v) Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager configuration; and (vi) hosting migration and DNS cutover support. Project timeline is ten (10) weeks from kickoff.

FEES AND PAYMENT

The total fee for the project described herein is fourteen thousand five hundred dollars ($14,500.00), consisting of:

Web design and development: $12,000.00
NexHealth integration setup: $1,500.00
SEO on-page setup: $1,000.00

The full invoice shall be issued upon execution of this Agreement. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. All amounts are in United States dollars.

If payment is not received within thirty (30) days of the due date, a late fee of seventy-five dollars ($75.00) per week shall apply to the outstanding balance until paid in full, with no grace period.

Developer shall transfer all access credentials, source files, and documentation upon receipt of payment in full.

HOSTING AND MAINTENANCE

Ongoing hosting and maintenance services are not included in this Agreement and are subject to a separate monthly retainer arrangement.
