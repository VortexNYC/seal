EVENT PLANNING SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: February 20, 2026

PARTIES

This Event Planning Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Luminary Events & Productions, LLC, a Florida limited liability company with its principal place of business at 200 SE 1st Street, Suite 400, Miami, FL 33131 ("Planner"), and

NovaTech Ventures, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 5901 N Andrews Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309 ("Client").

EVENT DESCRIPTION

Planner shall plan and execute Client's annual corporate leadership summit, scheduled for June 12–13, 2026, at the Fontainebleau Miami Beach, 4441 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33140. Services include full event concept development, vendor sourcing and management (audio-visual, catering, floral, security), attendee registration management, on-site coordination, and post-event reporting. Expected attendance is two hundred fifty (250) persons.

FEES AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE

The total planning and production fee for the event described herein is ninety-five thousand dollars ($95,000.00), denominated in United States dollars.

Payment shall be structured as follows:

(i) A deposit of twenty-five percent (25%) of the total fee, equal to twenty-three thousand seven hundred fifty dollars ($23,750.00), shall be due within five (5) business days of execution of this Agreement. This deposit is non-refundable and secures Planner's services for the contracted event dates.

(ii) The remaining balance of seventy-one thousand two hundred fifty dollars ($71,250.00), representing seventy-five percent (75%) of the total fee, shall be due no later than thirty (30) days prior to the event start date, i.e., by May 13, 2026.

Third-party vendor costs incurred on Client's behalf shall be invoiced separately at cost plus a fifteen percent (15%) coordination fee and shall be due upon receipt of invoice.

CANCELLATION

In the event of cancellation by Client less than sixty (60) days prior to the event, Client shall remain liable for the full contract amount. Cancellation sixty (60) or more days prior to the event forfeits the deposit only.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.
