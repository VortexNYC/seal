CUSTOM MANUFACTURING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

PARTIES

This Custom Manufacturing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Vortex Precision Fabrication, Inc., a Texas corporation with its principal place of business at 4200 Research Forest Drive, The Woodlands, TX 77381 ("Manufacturer"), and

Auris Medical Devices Corp., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 9801 Westheimer Road, Suite 1200, Houston, TX 77042 ("Buyer").

PRODUCT AND SPECIFICATIONS

Manufacturer shall design, fabricate, and deliver a batch of five hundred (500) units of the Auris MicroClamp Device (Model AUR-7), a Class II medical device component. All units shall be manufactured in conformance with the specifications set forth in Drawing Package DP-AUR7-Rev4, dated February 15, 2026, and shall comply with ISO 13485:2016 quality management requirements. Manufacturer shall provide a Certificate of Conformance and material traceability documentation with each delivery lot. Target delivery is ninety (90) days from receipt of the deposit payment.

PURCHASE PRICE AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE

The total purchase price for five hundred (500) units of the Auris MicroClamp Device is two million one hundred fifty thousand dollars ($2,150,000.00), consisting of:

Tooling and setup: $150,000.00
Per-unit fabrication (500 units × $4,000.00): $2,000,000.00

Payment shall be structured as follows:

(i) A deposit of twenty-five percent (25%) of the total purchase price, equal to five hundred thirty-seven thousand five hundred dollars ($537,500.00), is due within ten (10) business days of execution of this Agreement. The deposit covers tooling setup and shall be applied to the total purchase price.

(ii) The remaining balance of seventy-five percent (75%), equal to one million six hundred twelve thousand five hundred dollars ($1,612,500.00), is due within thirty (30) days of delivery and acceptance of the completed units at Buyer's designated facility.

All amounts are denominated in United States dollars. Late payment on the balance shall accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month from the due date, with no grace period.

QUALITY AND INSPECTION

Buyer shall have fifteen (15) business days following delivery to inspect goods and notify Manufacturer of any non-conforming units. Manufacturer shall replace or credit non-conforming units at no additional charge.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
