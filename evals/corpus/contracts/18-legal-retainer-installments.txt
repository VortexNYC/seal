LEGAL RETAINER AGREEMENT (INSTALLMENT BASIS)

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

PARTIES

This Legal Retainer Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Okafor & Singh LLP, a limited liability partnership with its principal place of business at 800 Third Avenue, 28th Floor, New York, NY 10022 ("Firm"), and

BlueSky Renewable Energy LLC, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1040 Avenue of the Americas, New York, NY 10018 ("Client").

SCOPE OF RETAINER

Firm shall provide Client with ongoing legal counsel in connection with Client's portfolio of solar and wind energy project development activities. Services shall include: contract drafting and negotiation (PPAs, EPC agreements, interconnection agreements), regulatory advice at state and federal levels, land use and permitting counsel, financing documentation review, and general corporate counsel as requested. The retainer shall cover a minimum of sixty (60) attorney hours per month. Hours beyond the minimum shall be billed at Firm's standard rates.

RETAINER FEE AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The total retainer fee for the initial six-month term (March 1, 2026 through August 31, 2026) is one hundred twenty thousand dollars ($120,000.00), denominated in United States dollars. Client elects to pay this amount in six (6) equal weekly installments, with each installment of twenty thousand dollars ($20,000.00) due every seven (7) days beginning March 8, 2026, as set forth below:

Week 1: $20,000.00 — due March 8, 2026
Week 2: $20,000.00 — due March 15, 2026
Week 3: $20,000.00 — due March 22, 2026
Week 4: $20,000.00 — due March 29, 2026
Week 5: $20,000.00 — due April 5, 2026
Week 6: $20,000.00 — due April 12, 2026

Each installment invoice shall be issued two (2) business days before the due date. All amounts are denominated in United States dollars.

RENEWAL

Following the initial six-month term, the retainer shall renew on a month-to-month basis at the rate of twenty thousand dollars ($20,000.00) per month, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
