INTEGRATED MARKETING CAMPAIGN AGREEMENT

Effective Date: February 15, 2026

PARTIES

This Integrated Marketing Campaign Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Archway Media Group, Inc., a Georgia corporation with its principal place of business at 1700 Peachtree Street NW, Suite 800, Atlanta, GA 30309 ("Agency"), and

Silverstone Realty Group, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company with its principal place of business at 3525 Piedmont Road NE, Atlanta, GA 30305 ("Client").

CAMPAIGN SCOPE

Agency shall plan and execute a twelve-week integrated marketing campaign in support of Client's new residential development, Silverstone Reserve, targeting affluent buyers aged 35–60 in the Metro Atlanta market. Campaign elements include: (i) digital advertising across Meta, Google Display, and programmatic channels; (ii) three (3) produced video spots (30-second and 15-second cuts); (iii) landing page design and A/B testing; (iv) email nurture sequence (6 emails); (v) bi-weekly performance reporting and optimization; and (vi) photography content for social channels (two shoot days).

CAMPAIGN FEE AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The total campaign fee is seventy-two thousand dollars ($72,000.00), denominated in United States dollars, exclusive of media spend (which shall be managed by Agency and billed at cost on a separate pass-through invoice). The campaign fee shall be paid in three (3) equal monthly installments of twenty-four thousand dollars ($24,000.00) each, due on the following dates:

Installment 1: $24,000.00 — due February 28, 2026
Installment 2: $24,000.00 — due March 28, 2026
Installment 3: $24,000.00 — due April 28, 2026

Each installment invoice shall be issued ninety (90) days before its due date to allow for Client's internal procurement and budget approval cycle. Each invoice is due ninety (90) days from the date of issuance. A late payment fee of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month of the outstanding installment balance shall apply to any payment not received within ninety (90) days of the late fee trigger date (i.e., ninety (90) days grace period from the invoice due date before late charges begin to accrue).

MEDIA SPEND

Client shall fund a dedicated media spend account with a minimum of thirty thousand dollars ($30,000.00) prior to campaign launch. Unused media spend shall be refunded within thirty (30) days of campaign completion.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
