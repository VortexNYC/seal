PARTIES

This Venue Booking and Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

The Grand Terrace at Ashfield Park Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 12456789), trading as "The Grand Terrace," with its registered office at Ashfield Park, Oxfordshire OX29 8AA ("Venue"), and

Pemberton-Clarke Family Office Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 09234567), acting on behalf of Mr. and Mrs. George Pemberton-Clarke, with its registered office at 22 Hill Street, Mayfair, London W1J 5NA ("Client").

EVENT DESCRIPTION

Venue shall host an exclusive private dinner and celebration event for approximately one hundred twenty (120) guests on September 5, 2026, including exclusive use of the Great Hall and East Terrace, in-house catering service for a five-course dinner with wine pairings, floristry and table décor per agreed design brief, valet parking coordination, and event staff (including butler service, silver-service waitstaff, and a head of house). An optional ceremony space is reserved subject to the add-on confirmation process in Schedule A.

FEES AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE

The total venue hire and services package fee is forty-eight thousand pounds sterling (£48,000.00).

Payment shall be structured as follows:

(i) A non-refundable booking deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the total fee, equal to twenty-four thousand pounds sterling (£24,000.00), is due within fourteen (14) days of execution of this Agreement. Receipt of the deposit confirms the event reservation.

(ii) The remaining balance of fifty percent (50%), equal to twenty-four thousand pounds sterling (£24,000.00), is due no later than sixty (60) days prior to the event date, i.e., by July 7, 2026.

Payments shall be made by bank transfer in pounds sterling.

CANCELLATION

If Client cancels this Agreement more than ninety (90) days before the event, the deposit is forfeited. If Client cancels within ninety (90) days of the event, the full contract amount shall be retained by Venue.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
