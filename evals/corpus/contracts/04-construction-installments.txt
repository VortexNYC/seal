CONSTRUCTION SERVICES CONTRACT

Effective Date: March 5, 2026

PARTIES

This Construction Services Contract ("Contract") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Broadstone General Contractors, Inc., a California corporation licensed under CSLB License No. 892341, with its principal office at 3340 Harbor Boulevard, Costa Mesa, CA 92626 ("Contractor"), and

Pacific Rim Hotel Group, LLC, a California limited liability company with its principal office at 550 South Flower Street, Suite 800, Los Angeles, CA 90071 ("Owner").

SCOPE OF WORK

Contractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to complete the tenant improvement buildout of floors 12 through 14 at Owner's property located at 720 Wilshire Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90017. The work includes demolition of existing partitions, installation of new MEP rough-in, framing, drywall, flooring, millwork, and finishes as specified in Architectural Drawing Set Rev. 3, dated February 20, 2026, which is incorporated herein by reference.

CONTRACT SUM AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE

The total contract sum for the work described herein is one million two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($1,250,000.00). This amount shall be paid in six (6) equal monthly installments of two hundred eight thousand three hundred thirty-three dollars and thirty-three cents ($208,333.33) each, with the first installment due on April 1, 2026 and each subsequent installment due on the first day of each month thereafter, through September 1, 2026. All amounts are denominated in United States dollars.

In the event any installment payment is not received within ten (10) days of its due date, Owner shall pay a late fee of two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00) for each week or partial week the payment remains outstanding beyond the grace period.

Owner shall have the right to withhold payment only where there is a bona fide dispute regarding the quality of work completed, and only in proportion to the disputed portion. Contractor may suspend work after fifteen (15) days of non-payment.

CHANGE ORDERS

No change to the contract sum or scope shall be binding unless evidenced by a written change order signed by both parties prior to commencement of the changed work.

GOVERNING LAW

This Contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
