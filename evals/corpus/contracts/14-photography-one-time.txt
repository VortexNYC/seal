EDITORIAL PHOTOGRAPHY SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

PARTIES

This Editorial Photography Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Erik Lund, a sole proprietor doing business as Lund Visual Media, with a principal place of business at 322 N 1st Avenue, Suite 200, Minneapolis, MN 55401 ("Photographer"), and

Nordic Home Furnishings, Inc., a Minnesota corporation with its principal place of business at 7200 France Avenue South, Edina, MN 55435 ("Client").

SCOPE OF SERVICES

Photographer shall provide a half-day editorial photography session at Client's Edina showroom for use in Client's internal employee magazine and intranet. Services include two (2) hours of on-site photography covering executive team headshots (up to eight individuals), light retouching of selected images, and delivery of final files in JPEG format via cloud link within five (5) business days of the session date. The session is scheduled for April 3, 2026.

FEES AND PAYMENT

Client shall pay Photographer as follows:

Session fee (half-day, up to 2 hours on-site): $375.00
Retouching and delivery (up to 8 selected images): $200.00
File licensing fee (internal editorial use, 3 years): $125.00

Total: $700.00

The invoice shall be issued upon execution of this Agreement and is due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Payment shall be made by check or ACH transfer.

Photographer shall not be obligated to deliver final images until payment has been received in full. No late fee is applicable under this Agreement.

All amounts are in United States dollars.

LICENSE

The license granted herein is limited to internal editorial use (employee magazine, intranet, internal presentations) for a period of three (3) years from the session date. Any commercial or external use shall require a separate licensing agreement and additional fee.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
