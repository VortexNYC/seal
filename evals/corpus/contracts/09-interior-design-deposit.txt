INTERIOR DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: March 10, 2026

PARTIES

This Interior Design Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Studio Eleven Interiors Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 11234567), with its registered office at 14 Redchurch Street, London E2 7DP ("Designer"), and

Aldgate Square Developments PLC, a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 08563421), with its registered office at 88 Wood Street, London EC2V 7RS ("Client").

PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Designer shall provide full interior design services for three (3) model apartment units (Units 4A, 4B, and 4C) at Client's residential development located at 22 Aldgate High Street, London EC3N 1AL. Services shall include space planning, mood board and concept development, specification of all furniture, fixtures, and finishes, procurement management, and installation oversight. Project completion is targeted for no later than June 30, 2026.

DESIGN FEES AND PAYMENT STRUCTURE

The total design fee for this project is thirty thousand pounds sterling (£30,000.00).

Payment shall be structured as follows:

(i) A deposit of fifty percent (50%) of the total design fee, equal to fifteen thousand pounds sterling (£15,000.00), is due within seven (7) business days of execution of this Agreement. The deposit secures Designer's availability and project start, and is non-refundable if Client cancels the project after the deposit has been received.

(ii) The balance of fifty percent (50%), equal to fifteen thousand pounds sterling (£15,000.00), is due within thirty (30) days of practical completion and handover of all three units, or no later than July 15, 2026, whichever is earlier.

Procurement and installation costs for furniture, fixtures, and finishes shall be invoiced separately based on actual costs plus a fifteen percent (15%) procurement handling fee, with payment due on receipt of each such invoice.

No late fee is specified in this Agreement; however, Designer reserves the right to suspend services if any payment is more than fourteen (14) days overdue.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
