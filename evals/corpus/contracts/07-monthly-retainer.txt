MARKETING RETAINER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

PARTIES

This Marketing Retainer Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Vanguard Creative Studio, Inc., a New York corporation with its principal place of business at 540 West 26th Street, New York, NY 10001 ("Agency"), and

Rosenthal Consumer Brands LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company with its principal place of business at 100 Overlook Center, Princeton, NJ 08540 ("Client").

RETAINER SCOPE

Agency shall provide Client with a dedicated team allocation of up to eighty (80) hours per calendar month, covering the following service areas: brand strategy and positioning, paid media planning and management (Meta, Google, TikTok), creative concepting, copywriting for digital channels, and monthly performance reporting. Hours not utilized within a calendar month shall not roll over or accrue credit.

Additional hours beyond the monthly allocation may be authorized by Client at Agency's standard blended rate of two hundred twenty-five dollars ($225.00) per hour.

PAYMENT TERMS

Client shall pay Agency a monthly retainer fee of twelve thousand dollars ($12,000.00) per month. The retainer shall be invoiced on the first business day of each month and is due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. The first invoice shall cover the period from March 1 through March 31, 2026, and shall be due by March 16, 2026.

If payment is not received within fifteen (15) days of the due date, Agency may, at its discretion, pause all active campaign management and suspend deliverables until the account is brought current. No late fee applies.

All amounts are in United States dollars.

TERM

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue on a month-to-month basis. Either party may terminate the Agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice. Client shall remain responsible for payment of all fees incurred through the end of the notice period.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
