SOFTWARE MAINTENANCE AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

PARTIES

This Software Maintenance and Support Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

CatalystOps Technologies Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 10345678), with its registered office at Second Floor, 1 Fore Street Avenue, London EC2Y 9DT ("Provider"), and

Pemberton Wealth Management (Americas), Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1251 Avenue of the Americas, New York, NY 10020 ("Client").

SERVICES

Provider shall maintain and support Client's instance of the Catalyst Portfolio Engine, version 6.x, including: (i) corrective maintenance to address software defects reported by Client within agreed severity-based response times; (ii) minor functional updates and patches; (iii) compatibility maintenance for operating system and infrastructure updates; and (iv) a dedicated support helpdesk available Monday through Friday, 08:00–18:00 GMT, with a four-hour response time for Priority 1 issues.

PAYMENT TERMS

Client shall pay Provider a bi-monthly maintenance and support fee of three thousand two hundred fifty United States dollars ($3,250.00) per two-month period. Invoices shall be issued on the first business day of every other month, commencing April 1, 2026, and are due within sixty (60) days of the invoice date. All amounts are denominated in United States dollars.

If any invoice remains unpaid after thirty (30) days beyond the due date, Provider may, upon ten (10) days' written notice, suspend support services and withhold access to the support helpdesk until all outstanding amounts have been paid in full. No formal late fee applies, but interest shall accrue at four percent (4%) per annum on any overdue balance.

TERM

This Agreement shall continue for an initial period of twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date and shall renew automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party provides sixty (60) days' prior written notice of non-renewal.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
