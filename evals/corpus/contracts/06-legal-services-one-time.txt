LEGAL SERVICES ENGAGEMENT LETTER

Effective Date: January 28, 2026

PARTIES

This Legal Services Engagement Letter ("Engagement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

Whitmore & Associates LLP, a limited liability partnership organized under the laws of England and Wales, with its registered office at 30 St Mary Axe, London EC3A 8EP ("Firm"), and

Carrington Industrial Holdings Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 09871234), with its registered office at One Canada Square, London E14 5AB ("Client").

SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT

The Firm shall represent Client in connection with the negotiation, drafting, and execution of a cross-border asset purchase agreement relating to Client's proposed acquisition of Dortmund Precision Parts GmbH ("Transaction"). Services shall include: (i) due diligence review of target company agreements and corporate records; (ii) drafting and negotiating the asset purchase agreement and ancillary transaction documents; (iii) regulatory filing advisory; and (iv) closing coordination. This engagement does not include post-closing integration support, which shall be subject to a separate engagement.

FEES AND PAYMENT

The Firm shall charge Client a fixed fee of forty-five thousand pounds sterling (£45,000.00) for the engagement described above. This fixed fee covers all attorney time through transaction closing and includes internal disbursements such as document management and internal research services. Third-party disbursements (filing fees, external expert costs) shall be invoiced separately at cost.

The fixed fee shall be invoiced upon execution of this Engagement and is due within sixty (60) days of the invoice date. Payment shall be made in pounds sterling by electronic transfer to the Firm's client account, details of which are set forth on the invoice.

CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVILEGE

All communications and work product generated under this Engagement shall be protected by legal professional privilege to the fullest extent permitted by law.

GOVERNING LAW

This Engagement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
